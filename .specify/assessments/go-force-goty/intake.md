## Restated

Add a `Go Force` page/module to the Ako Chronicles blog site to facilitate a live, multi-player, three-round process for selecting Game of the Year nominees from an admin-seeded annual list. The game master operates the roulette, and players use their own screens to vote `go` or `no-go` as each game is selected without replacement. The per-player `go` allowance decreases from three to one; every player must use their Round 3 `go`, and any game receiving at least one `go` advances. After round 3, finalists appear in a one-vote-per-player GOTY ballot with optional remarks; if the vote ties, all tied games share the title.

## Clarifications Received

- The player count is not predetermined. It is fixed when a session starts, and no additional players can join after the start.
- The admin manually adds the eligible games for the year using an admin-facing maintenance screen.
- Everyone participates in one shared live session at the same time.
- The session is livestreamed to the user's channel. Players join the session through Discord, and the game master shares their screen with everyone.
- Players cannot see vote results on their own screens; results are displayed on the facilitator/game master's screen, which is shared with the group.
- The user is the game master and operates the roulette. Players use their own screens to cast votes; their votes are hidden from other players.
- A round ends after every game in that round's roulette has been selected once. Unused `go` allowances are forfeited.
- A game advances when at least one player votes `go`; unanimity is not required.
- Each selected game is removed from the roulette for that round so it cannot be selected again during that round.
- Every player is required to use their one `go` vote in round 3.
- After round 3, games that received at least one `go` become GOTY finalists.
- The final GOTY ballot lists the finalists. Each player casts one vote and may add a remark.
- If the final ballot is tied, all tied games share the Game Ako GOTY title; there is no additional runoff to choose a single winner.
- The site should display vote history, round results, and the final shortlist.
- Go Force should be ready before the user-reported December 10, 2026 Game Awards date.
- The user wants to proceed regardless of demonstrated audience demand, with the intent of creating demand through the content.
- Players will use existing Game Ako accounts to vote.
