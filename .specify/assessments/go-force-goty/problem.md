# Problem Definition: Game Ako Game of the Year selection

- **Slug**: go-force-goty
- **Created**: 2026-11-20
- **Inputs used**: intake.md; research.md not present

## Problem Statement

Game Ako currently has no process for selecting its Game of the Year, and the community wants to establish one for its content. Players need a clear way to determine the annual GOTY result, and the audience needs access to the voting outcome after the live event.

## Affected Users & Stakeholders

- **Users**: Game Ako players — participate in choosing the year's GOTY during a live session.
- **Stakeholders**: Game master/admin (the user) — curates eligible games and facilitates the session.
- **Stakeholders**: Game Ako channel audience — watches the streamed session and views its published outcome.

## Goals

- Establish a repeatable annual process that produces a GOTY result from admin-curated eligible games and player votes.
- Let a player group of variable size participate in one live session, with membership fixed when the session begins.
- Make the progression from eligible games to finalists and the final GOTY result understandable to participants and viewers.
- Publish the voting results, round outcomes, vote history, and final shortlist on a separate page after the live session.
- Preserve the possibility of multiple co-winners when the final ballot is tied.

## Non-Goals

- Creating or hosting a video game; `Go Force` is an activity for selecting GOTY.
- Automatically choosing or importing eligible games; the admin manually curates the annual list.
- Supporting participants joining after the session has started.
- Requiring a single GOTY winner when the final ballot is tied.
- Supporting asynchronous participation; the intended activity is a shared live session.
- Publishing the results page before the live show or selection process is complete.

## Success Metrics

- **Completed-session outcome rate**: share of completed sessions that produce at least one named GOTY winner (target: 100%; baseline: no existing process).
- **Required-vote completion rate**: share of expected player vote opportunities submitted during the session (target: at least 80%; baseline: unknown).
- **Post-session results availability**: share of completed sessions with a separate results page available after the event (target: 100%; baseline: no existing process).

## Cost of Inaction

Without a selection process, Game Ako will continue without a formal annual GOTY outcome for its content. The community will not have the intended vote-based event or a post-session results page documenting its outcome.
